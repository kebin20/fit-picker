Only exported model/data files were imported. No GPL application, plugin or build code from that project is included in Fit Picker. Fit Picker's loader, material handling, pose bake and coverage filtering are implemented separately.

The shipped GLBs contain a generic adult male body, short hair, a T-shirt, shirt, hoodie/sweater base, trousers, shorts, shoes, jacket, coat and an underlayer. The preparation script applies an adult male morph and a relaxed standing pose, bakes skin/morph deformation, removes unused morph data, and embeds textures capped at 1024 pixels. `manifest.json` lists the actual files and their sizes.

These are generic fitting templates, not scans of Kevin or measured models of his clothes. Several categories share a silhouette: trouser cuts and sneaker styles are approximate; bomber, puffer and overshirt styles initially use the fitted jacket template. Sweaters omit the hood component, short-sleeve shirts shorten the authored sleeves, and the coat hem is adapted to knee length. Exact brand details, garment prints and physical sizing are not reproduced. Product photographs and the original guides remain the references for item identity.
